Privacy & SMS Terms

Last updated 17 August 2026

Ephoria is a hiring-automation service used by home care agencies to answer, screen and schedule caregiver applicants over text message. This page explains what we collect, why, and how anyone receiving our messages can stop them.

The short version

  • We text caregiver applicants on behalf of the agency they applied to, and only after they applied.
  • Reply STOP to any message to stop permanently. Reply HELP for help.
  • We never sell or rent personal information, and we never share phone numbers with advertisers.
  • The demo on our home page runs entirely in your browser. It sends no text and stores nothing.

Who we are

Ephoria is operated by Badal Kariwal. For any question about this policy, or to request deletion of your data, email kariwalbadal@gmail.com and we will respond within 30 days.

Information we collect

From caregiver applicants. When someone applies to a job at an agency that uses Ephoria, we receive the information they submitted with that application — typically name and mobile number — from the agency or its job board. During the text conversation we also record their answers to the agency's screening questions (availability, transportation, certification, experience, start date, desired hours) and the messages exchanged.

From agency operators. Name, email address, agency name, and the screening questions and scheduling preferences you configure.

From website visitors. The screening demo on our home page runs entirely in your browser. Anything typed into it stays on your device, is never transmitted to us, and is discarded when you close the tab. The cost calculator works the same way. We do not use advertising cookies or third-party tracking pixels. Our web fonts are served by Google Fonts, which receives your IP address as part of that request.

How we use it

  • To send and receive the screening conversation on the agency's behalf.
  • To schedule interviews and send interview reminders.
  • To pass the applicant's answers and transcript to the hiring agency.
  • To operate, secure and debug the service.

We do not use applicant data to train third-party models, we do not sell it, and we do not use it to market anything to applicants.

SMS messaging terms

What the messages are. Recurring, conversational messages relating to a job application: screening questions, interview scheduling, interview reminders, and onboarding follow-ups for candidates who are hired.

Consent. Messages are sent only to people who applied for a role at the agency named in the message. Consent is not a condition of employment or of any purchase, and an applicant who opts out is not disadvantaged in their application.

Frequency. It varies with the conversation. If an applicant does not reply, we send at most two follow-ups over 48 hours and then stop contacting them.

Opting out. Reply STOP to any message. This takes effect immediately and permanently, we send no further message of any kind to that number, and we send no confirmation reply. Reply HELP for assistance, or email us at the address above.

Hours. No message is sent before 8:00am or after 9:00pm in the recipient's local time zone.

Cost. Message and data rates may apply. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Automation disclosure. The first message in every conversation states that it comes from an automated scheduling assistant. Our software never claims to be a human being.

Who we share it with

We share applicant information with the hiring agency the person applied to, which is the entire point of the service. We also use a small number of processors to operate it: Twilio (message delivery), Google Cloud (hosting and the language model that interprets free-text replies), and our email provider. Each receives only what it needs to perform its function. We disclose information to law enforcement only where legally required.

How long we keep it

Applicant records and transcripts are retained for the duration of the agency's engagement with us and for up to 24 months afterwards, then deleted. Opt-out records are kept indefinitely — we must remember that a number opted out in order to honour it. Anyone may request earlier deletion by emailing us.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or port your personal information, and to opt out of its sale or sharing — we do not sell or share it in the first place. California residents have these rights under the CCPA as amended by the CPRA, and we will not discriminate against anyone for exercising them. Email us to make a request; we may need to verify your identity first.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit. Access is limited to the people who need it to run the service. Phone numbers are redacted in our operational logs. No system is perfectly secure, and we make no guarantee that it is.

Children

The service is for adults applying for employment. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 16.

Changes

If we change this policy materially we will update the date at the top and, where the change affects messaging practices, notify affected agencies directly.
