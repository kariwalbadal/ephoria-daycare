How it works

From application to interview, while you run your agency.

01

They apply

From Indeed, your website, Facebook — anywhere. Webhook, form, or even a pasted CSV. No ATS integration required to start.

02

Ephoria texts first

Within 60 seconds, every applicant gets a text with your agency's name on it. Six short screening questions, one at a time — availability, transport, certification, experience.

03

Interviews appear on your calendar

Qualified applicants pick from three real slots. Booked, confirmed, reminded the day before and the day of. Nudges continue through their first shift.

It never claims to be human.

Asked directly, it says exactly what it is: your scheduling assistant, able to book right now.

It knows when to stop.

STOP always means stop. Confused twice, asking for a person, anything urgent — the conversation is flagged to you and the machine steps aside.

It keeps civilized hours.

No messages before 8am or after 9pm in the applicant's own timezone. Ever.
