The questions you're actually asking.

Turnover is 75%. Do I pay $249 again every time someone quits?

Yes — $249 each time a caregiver starts a first shift. That's exactly why the price is per-hire and not per-month: in the months you hire nobody, you pay nothing. A monthly tool bills you through your quiet season and through every candidate who ghosts. Compare it to what you already spend to fill one seat — job board spend, the hours your scheduler loses to voicemail, and the client revenue waiting on that hire.

What do I actually see? Do I have to learn new software?

No. You get the record on the right of the demo above — name, their answers to your questions, the full transcript, and the booked time — delivered the way you already work: email, text, or a shared sheet. There is no dashboard you have to log into, and nothing changes about how you post jobs.

Whose phone number does it text from?

A number we register and operate for your agency, carrier-approved for business messaging. Replies route to the screener; anything it can't handle is handed to you with the whole conversation attached. You never have to register anything with a carrier yourself — that process is slow and unpleasant, and it's the part we absorb.

Does it work with Indeed / my ATS / my careers page?

Yes. Applicants reach the screener from wherever they already come from — Indeed, your website form, a spreadsheet, or a forwarded email. We build the connector for your setup during the pilot. You keep posting jobs exactly the way you do today.

What happens when it doesn't understand someone?

It stops guessing. After two unclear replies — or the moment anyone asks for a person, sounds distressed, or asks something it wasn't given an answer for — it stops screening, tells them a human will follow up, and flags the conversation to you. It never invents an answer about pay, hours, or clients. Try it in the demo above: type "banana" twice, or ask for a real person.

What about applicants who call instead of texting? We miss a lot of calls.

That's the same leak in a different pipe, and it's usually the bigger one — a missed call at 4pm on a Friday is gone. We can put a number in front of it that picks up every time: it catches the call you couldn't answer, texts them straight back, and runs the same screening conversation you saw above. If they'd rather speak to someone, it takes the reason and a time and hands it to you. This is a custom piece, not a checkbox — how calls should be caught depends on your phone setup, so we scope it on the fit call and quote it for your agency.

My agency does things differently. Can it be built around us?

That's the normal case, not the exception. Different screening bar, an extra compliance question, Spanish-speaking applicants, a scheduler who wants everything in one morning email, a referral source that needs its own flow — all of it is buildable, and we'd rather fit the software to how you already work than ask your team to change. Tell us what's awkward about your setup on the fit call; custom scope is quoted per agency on top of the pricing above.

Is this a bot pretending to be a person?

No, and it never will be. The first message says it's the automated scheduling assistant for your agency. Applicants who ask are told plainly. We think the agencies that pretend otherwise are going to regret it, and we'd rather not be one of them.
